## Discord Developer Communities

I just open-sourced PromptGuild, a TypeScript tool for building Discord servers from natural-language prompts.

It generates a complete server blueprint, shows a dry-run preview, and provisions the server through an official Discord bot using `discord.js`.

It supports roles, categories, channels, permission overwrites, starter messages, AutoMod rules, webhooks, reusable templates, OpenAI, Ollama/local models, auto mode, and deterministic local templates.

No self-bots, no user-token automation, and no scraping.

I am looking for contributors who like Discord bots, TypeScript, AI agents, local AI, and community tooling.

Repo: https://github.com/MichaelMattern/PromptGuild
